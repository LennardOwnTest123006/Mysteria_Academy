# NACHTGLAS 1.01 — Visual Direction & Shot List

**Aspect:** 2.39:1 · **Frame rate:** 24 fps · **Colour:** ACES, D55 base, show LUT „NG_Valley_01"

---

## 1. THE THREE LIGHTS

The whole episode is legible from lighting alone. Only three light characters exist:

| | Colour temp | Quality | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NACHTGLAS** | 2200 K amber | Soft-edged, visible glow ring, warm falloff, gentle flicker at 0.4 Hz | Safety. Memory. The town as it was. |
| **NEU-LICHT (LED)** | 5600 K white | Hard edge, no glow ring, dead flat, zero flicker | Progress that doesn't work. Renders skin grey, fog like paper. |
| **DAS GLASLICHT** | #7FE3C8 → #1B4D4A | Internal, sourceless, no falloff, no shadow | The supernatural. Used 11 times in the episode. Never for anything mundane. |

**Rule:** never mix Nachtglas amber and LED white in the same practical. When both are in
frame (Sc. 3, Sc. 19) they are staged on opposite sides of the axis so the cut between them
is a temperature cut.

---

## 2. NIGHT EXTERIOR GRAMMAR — „ISLANDS AND DARK"

Every night exterior is blocked as **islands of light with dark between them**. Before any
dialogue explains the rule, the frame has already taught it:

- Characters are staged *inside* pools of lamplight; when they move between them, hold the
  shot wide enough that we see them cross the dark.
- The Löscher is never staged in an island. Ever. He is only ever in the between.
- Depth is built with three fog planes: near (2–4 m, drifting, lit from behind), mid
  (12–25 m, holding lamp shafts), far (60 m+, flat, killing the horizon).

---

## 3. THE LÖSCHER — HARD ANIMATION RULES

**These are inviolable. They are the character.**

1. **He is never animated in locomotion.** No steps, no walk cycle, no drift. He changes
   position only across (a) a cut, (b) a character's blink, (c) a light source sweeping off
   and back, (d) a foreground wipe.
2. **He does not react.** No head turns toward speakers, no flinch, no anticipation. The only
   part of him that animates is the small door of the Leerlaterne and, once, the fingers.
3. **His face-pane holds a live reflection of whoever is looking**, dimmed 40 % and delayed
   9 frames. That delay is the single scariest asset in the show — budget for it.
4. **Never in close-up in Episode 1.** Minimum framing: full figure, occupying no more than
   28 % of frame height. First close-up is held for 3.04.
5. **Light bends toward the Leerlaterne** — implemented as a physical volumetric distortion
   field, not a post effect, so the bend is correct from every angle and interacts with fog.
6. **He never touches anyone.** Not once, ever, in the series.

---

## 4. THE MARBLE-VISION LOOK (11 uses)

When a character raises Nachtglas to their eye, the world does not "cut" to a vision. It
**transitions optically inside the shot**, over 14 frames:

- Circular vignette blooms from centre, edge softened with a real glass-caustic ring.
- Slight barrel distortion and a 3 % green-cyan channel shift.
- Fog density drops 60 %; ambient occlusion deepens; the **seams** appear — thin bright
  filaments of #7FE3C8 running lamp to lamp, animated with a slow 3 s travelling pulse.
- Sound simultaneously: high-shelf lift and the "glass tone" (see 03_SOUND_DESIGN §4).
- **The taken people are rendered at full fidelity, not as ghosts.** They are solid, clothed,
  lit blue-green, and they hold eye contact. Translucency would make them safe. They are not.

## 5. CAMERA POLICY

| Sequence | Package | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open aerial (Sc. 1) | 35 mm, deep stop | Continuous descent, 41 s, one shot |
| Kosta / bypass (Sc. 2) | 40 mm, T2.0 | Locked, then two slow push-ins, no handheld — his fear is stillness |
| Marktplatz day (Sc. 3–4) | 27 mm handheld | Loose, warm, social; the only genuinely "light" scene |
| Classroom (Sc. 5, 7) | 50 mm / 85 mm | Locked. Sc. 7 is Sc. 5 repeated with one desk removed — **identical camera positions**, so the audience feels the wrongness before finding it |
| Elif's taking (Sc. 6) | 50 mm | **Locked-off wide, 20 m back, no move at all.** The camera's refusal to help is the horror |
| War room (Sc. 10) | 32 mm | Slow orbit around the crate-table, one full revolution across the scene |
| Uhlengasse rescue (Sc. 15–16) | 24–35 mm handheld | Breathing, operator-following; the only handheld in the third act |
| The corridor build (Sc. 16) | 24 mm | One continuous 48-second Steadicam walk down the lane |
| Tarek at the window (Sc. 20) | 100 mm, T1.8 | Locked, shallow — his face and a reflection, nothing else in focus |
| The relight (Sc. 21) | 21 mm, low | Static during black, then a 4-second rise as the ring closes |
| Final crane (Sc. 24) | 27 mm → 14 mm | Crane up 60 m, then push *through* the marble in one unbroken move |

**Average shot length: 5.6 s. No cut shorter than 12 frames anywhere in the episode.**
Reveals are made by camera move, never by cut.

## 6. CONTINUITY LOCK LIST

The following are fixed for the entire episode and must be checked every shot:

- **Juno:** claw clip, two escaped curls at the LEFT temple; carabiner at LEFT belt loop;
  marble in RIGHT jacket pocket for Sc. 4–23, in her RIGHT HAND from Sc. 12 on. Jacket
  unzipped throughout; sleeves pushed to the elbow from Sc. 15 onward.
- **Tarek:** rain jacket **never zipped**. Headphones around neck in every scene except
  Sc. 20–22, where they are gone (dropped in Sc. 16 — visible on the cobbles in Sc. 23).
- **Nell:** hood UP outdoors, DOWN indoors, without exception. Sketchbook rubber band is
  **red**. Charcoal on right hand only.
- **Basti:** slides-and-socks in Sc. 5 only; trainers from Sc. 9 on. Headlamp pushed up on
  forehead when off, over eyes when on. Battery visibly dimming from Sc. 16 to Sc. 21.
- **Kosta:** name patch illegible in every shot. Thermos in the top box; it is still there,
  and rattles, in Sc. 23. Moped's right mirror is taped — and stays taped.
- **Pepe:** ONE glove, LEFT hand, all episode, including after the rescue.
- **Lantern 47:** modern head present Sc. 6 → removed Sc. 19 → old globe + crocodile clips
  Sc. 21 onward. The tape job must be identical in Sc. 21, 22, 23 and 24.
- **Marbles:** Juno's is the smoothest and palest. Nell's is on a leather cord. Kosta's has
  one flat chipped facet. Tarek's is in the recorder pouch. Basti's is a *bead*, drilled,
  on a lanyard. These five are visually distinguishable in close-up and must never swap.
