# NACHTGLAS — Main Title Sequence
**Duration 0:40 · 24 fps · 2.39:1 · Music: „NACHTGLAS — Main Title", 7/8**

The title sequence is one continuous camera move. There is not a single cut in it.

| TC | Shot |
|---|---|
| 00:00 | Black. A low glass tone. One orange point of light, tiny, centre frame. |
| 00:03 | The point resolves: **molten glass gathering on the end of a blowpipe**, turning slowly. Macro, T1.4, the background a furnace-orange nothing. The gather has the exact viscosity of honey and drips once. |
| 00:07 | Breath. A soundless, enormous inhale — and the gather **swells**. Camera pulls back as the globe grows to fill frame. |
| 00:11 | The globe's surface becomes a mirror. Camera pushes into the curve of it. |
| 00:13 | **JUNO** slides across the curve — jaw set, pushing a curl off her face. She is gone in 1.5 s. |
| 00:15 | **TAREK** — glasses full of lamplight, mouth already moving, hands going. |
| 00:18 | **NELL** — hood up, charcoal-black fingers lifting a pencil toward us. |
| 00:21 | **BASTI** — headlamp clicks on, and he grins straight down the lens. The one warm second. |
| 00:24 | **KOSTA** — perfectly still, not looking at us. Looking at something behind us. |
| 00:27 | Camera pulls back out of the curve. The globe **cools**: orange → grey → pale blue-green. Frost creeps in from the poles. |
| 00:31 | Inside the cooled glass, a fog. Inside the fog, for eight frames only, the silhouette of a lane of lanterns going out one by one. |
| 00:33 | **The crack.** A hairline fracture races across the glass from left to right — and as it travels it *is* the wordmark, letters cut out of the fracture as it goes. |
| 00:37 | **NACHTGLAS** complete, held. Beneath it, small: *Was das Licht vergisst, behält das Glas.* |
| 00:39 | A small amber flame ignites in the counter of the **A**. |
| 00:40 | Cut to black on the downbeat. |

**Design notes.** The wordmark (`brand/nachtglas_logo.svg`) is a geometric sans with flat
terminals, letterforms constructed from an 11-unit grid — eleven, because there are 111
lanterns, and because nobody will notice, and that is fine. The **A** carries the flame; the
**H** carries the fracture. The lockup never appears in white: it is always pale blue-green
(#BFE9DE) with a 2 px amber inner glow, so that the logo itself is a piece of Nachtglas
with a light caught in it.

**End card:** on the final smash-to-black of every episode, the wordmark appears alone,
un-animated, for 1.2 s, with a single struck glass note — the same note every time.
